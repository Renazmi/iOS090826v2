OBRA Creative Media Productions

Constitution and By-Laws of OBRA: CCS Creative Media Production Organization

Article I — OBRA Creative Media Productions
Section 1. OBRA Creative Media Productions
This organization shall be known as the OBRA CREATIVE MEDIA PRODUCTION. The use of the term OBRA in this document shall be understood to refer to the auxiliary student organization of 1Tera Organization of the College of Computer Studies of the Dominican College of Tarlac, Inc.

Section 2. OBRA Logo
2.1 The term “OBRA”
The term “obra” is derived from the Spanish word which means “work” or “labor”, encapsulating a broad spectrum of work, be in a work of art; artwork; body of work; masterpiece.
The group is dedicated to produce “OBRA” that is suitable for its users across diverse platforms, seeking to create a sense of community among its audience fostering loyalty through transparent, authentic, and meaningful interactions in the different social platforms.
2.2 The Film Reel Symbol
The symbol represents videos, still photos, music and other form of media products that will be produce by the members of the organization.
2.3 The Red Play Button
The triangle-shaped play button that point to the right indicating a sense of forward movement or progress towards interactivity and engagement.

Article II — Vision and Mission
Section 1. General Mission
The organization’s mission is to produce engaging and meaningful content that will inform audiences through the power of creative media production, committed in crafting compelling contents and fostering meaningful connections with our viewers.

Section 2. Vision
The organization shall be serving as the venue in creating content that not only captivates and entertains but also educates, inspire, and drive positive audience engagement.

Article III — Membership
Section 2. Right and Responsibilities of Members
2.1 Membership in the organization is open to individuals who share an interest and commitment to the vison and mission of the organization.
2.2 All members have the right to attend and participate in all the meetings of the members of the organization.
2.4 All members shall obey and comply with the by-laws, rules and regulations that may be promulgated by the organization from time to time.

Section 3. Good Standing and Expulsion of Members
3.1 Any member who pays all this dues, attends meetings set by the organization, and has not committed shall be considered members in good standing.
3.2 A member who has not been in good standing for two (2) consecutive semesters may be considered for expulsion.

Article IV — Meetings
Section 1. General Meetings
General meetings shall be held regularly depending on the urgency of the project production, and all members shall be notified in advance. These meetings will provide updates on projects, finances, and other important matters.

Section 2. Special Meetings
Special meetings of the members shall be called, as the need therefore arises, by the officers. The notice of every special meeting shall state briefly the purpose of the meeting. No other business shall be considered at such meeting except with the consent of the members present thereat. Notice of meeting may be waived verbally by any member attending it.

Section 3. Notice of Meetings
Notice of date, time, and place of the general and other meetings of the members shall be given personally or by electronic mail or by advertisements, at least one week before the date set for the meeting. In urgent cases, the notice may be communicated at least two (2) DAYS BEFORE THE MEETING – personally or through cellular communication or by announcements – when direct contact is not possible.

Section 4. Expenses
The council of Officers shall pre-set a ceiling for the expenses to be incurred in the preparation for, conduct of, and summation / documentation of project. The Officers shall also pre-set a ceiling for the expenses to be incurred in the preparation form, the conduct of and summation / documentation of every project.

Article V — Council
Section 1. Function
The 1Tera Officers will serve as the Council of Officers, and shall be the governing body of the organization and shall manage its affairs; i.e. the corporate powers of the organization shall be exercised, its business conducted and its property controlled by the Council.

Section 2. Term of Office
The council shall hold office for two (2) semesters beginning first Friday of July of the year they were elected and until their successors are duly elected and qualified.

Article VI — Officers
Section 1. Officers
The officers of the organization shall be comprised of a Content Producer, Content Director, Creative Writers, Photographers, Videographers, Animators, and Photo/Video Editors.

Section 2. Appointment
The appointment process for officers in the organization typically involves a series of steps to ensure that qualified individuals are selected to fill leadership roles.

Section 3. Application and Screening
Applicants for officer position should submit a written application or statement of intent and a portfolio, outlining their qualifications, experience, and vision for the role. An applicant may submit these documents to the 1Tera Officers. The Officers of 1Tera will serve as the screening committee that will screen the applicants to ensure they meet the eligibility criteria and qualifications outlined in this Constitution and Bylaws.

Section 3. Term of Office
An officer shall hold office for two (2) semesters beginning the month of the year he/she elected into his/her position and until his/her successor is duly elected.

Section 4. Succession
In the event that the officer is not able perform his/her duties, the President of 1Tera organization and with the approval of the Consultant shall appoint an Officer-in-Charge (OIC) or an Acting Officer from among themselves. The appointee shall serve during and absence of the officer.

Section 5. Vacancy
Should a vacancy arise in any one of the following positions in the organization– the members of the Council shall elect a replacement. The elected individual shall serve for the remainder of the term.

Article VII — Duties of the Officers
Section 1. Content Producer
A Content Producer plans, creates and produce various kinds of projects, like articles, content for websites and social media handles, AVPs, PubMat, posters, and advertising materials. He/she produce content based on the needs on a specific topic for a diverse audience.

Section 2. Creative Writer/s
He/she is assign in conceptualizing, writing and developing content materials. He/she coordinates with the Content Director in the production stages of the project to achieve desired results set by the team.

Section 3. Content Director
He/she coordinates all the aspects of the creation for every type of media content. He/she work together with writers, producers, actors and other media developers to harmonize all the activities in the pre-production, production and post-production stages.

Section 4. Photographers
He/she is responsible in capturing still images and work collaboratively with the producer, directors, writers, and other members of the organization. To develop concepts and employ various techniques in producing still images to achieve desired results set by the team.

Section 5. Videographers
He/she take and shoot video footage and work collaboratively with the producer, directors, writers, and other members of the organization. To develop concepts and employ various techniques in producing video footages to achieve desired results set by the team. He/she will also involve in post-production after video footages has been shot.

Section 6. Animators
He/she is skilled in creating and designing different animations used in cartoons, and other types of animated content.

Section 7. Photo/Video Editors
He/she is specialized in editing video footage. In the production stage of the project, he/she choose the video clips that apply to the story and make adjustments to enhance the visual aesthetics of the project.

Section 8. Graphic Designers
He/she is specialized in editing media elements such as text, images and video content. He/she will create visual concepts and materials to communicate information to audience.

Section 9. Consultant
He/she provide expert opinions, analysis and recommendation to the organization, developing strategies and offer guidance to the organization.

Article VIII — Organization’s Expenses
Section 1. Requisition of Budget for Projects
Budget requisition will be made 2 days prior to the project production. The requisition of the project will take the process of requesting, justifying and obtaining approval for financial expenditures related to project production to the Officers of 1Tera.

Section 2. Budget Development
The members of the organization may begin creating comprehensive budget for the project production. The budget should outline all expected expenses, including pre-production, production, post-production, and distribution costs. The requested expenses may be categorize into logical groups such as personnel, equipment, location, post-production, marketing, and miscellaneous cost.

Section 3. Expense Requisition Form
The members of the organization may request an expense requisition form to the 1Tera Officers that includes the following information: Date of request; Name of the person making the request; Description of the expense; Expense category; Amount Requested; Justification or reason for the expense; Supporting document (quotes, invoices, receipts); Project or Production phase which the expense is allocated.

Section 4. Review and Approval of Request
The 1Tera organization will review the requisition for accuracy, alignment with the budget, and appropriateness. They may also verify that the requested expense is essential to the project production. Once the expense requisition is approved, the treasurer of 1Tera organization will communicate to the person who made the request and will inform any specific guidelines or instructions for making the expenditure.

Section 5. Reporting and Closing Out Expenses
The member of the organization will provide expense report to the 1Tera organization. These reports should compare actual expenditures to the budget and explain any variances. After the production is complete, reconcile all expenses, ensure all outstanding bills are paid, and close out the budget.

Article IX — Projects and Project Production
Section 1. Types of Project
The organization can undertake a wide range of projects depending on their niche, expertise, and objectives. The following are the possible types of project to be developed by the organization:
1.1 Still Photo and Film Production – this includes creating feature films, documentaries, short films and taking and capturing still photos of an event.
1.2 Publication Materials and Digital Contents – this includes developing original pubmats, and digital content for online platforms.
1.3 Commercials and Advertising Campaigns – this includes producing television commercials, online advertisements, and promotional videos.
1.4 Music Videos – this includes producing music videos for musicians and bands.
1.5 Animation – this includes developing animated content, including 2D and 3D animation, for various purposes such as entertainment, education, and marketing.
1.6 Live Events and Streaming – this includes covering live events such as concerts, conferences, sports events, and theater productions.
1.7 Educational and Instructional Content – this includes developing educational videos, tutorials, and e-learning courses.

Article X — Amendments, Repeals and New By-Laws
Section 1
Any member of the organization may submit proposed amendments in writing to the 1Tera Officers.

Section 2
Such proposals shall be published and distributed to all members of the organization during its annual meetings.

Section 3
This constitution and by-laws may be amended by affirmation by at least two-thirds (2/3) of the votes officially present or represented at a general meeting at which a quorum of the general membership is present.

Section 4
Amendments duly approved shall take effect immediately.
